# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-16)

First release: converts a Teslascope export (zip or folder, several at once) into monthly TeslaFi CSV files for TeslaMate's importer. Drives and charges with their points, odometer, battery and charging curves. Tested on a 14-month Model 3 export imported into TeslaMate 4.3.0 with zero rejected rows.
